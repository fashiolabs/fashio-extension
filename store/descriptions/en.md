Image size checker and size chart maker for people who sell clothing online. Check a product page's photos and text against marketplace image guidance before buyers see it, and build a size chart for the listing in a minute.

IMAGE SIZE CHECKER
• Open any product page — your own store, a draft preview or a live listing — and click the Fashio icon.
• Every large product photo is listed with its pixel size (resolution), aspect ratio and file size.
• Pick a marketplace preset — Amazon, Etsy, Shopify, eBay or Zalando — and each image is checked against that marketplace's published image guidance: minimum and recommended size, maximum dimensions, aspect ratio and file size.
• The main image is checked for a white background and for how much of the frame the product fills.
• Finds images listed in the product's structured data that return an error, and photos with no alt text.
• Click a thumbnail to jump to that image on the page.
• Every rule links to the help page it came from. Where a number is commonly cited but not on a public help page (for example some Amazon figures), the extension says so and asks you to confirm it in Seller Central.

LISTING TEXT LENGTH
• Product title, page title, meta description and structured-data description, each with its character count.
• Checked against the published limit where the marketplace gives one, such as Etsy and eBay title limits and Shopify's search title and meta description.

SIZE CHART MAKER
• Women's US / UK / EU / FR / IT / JP or men's UK / EU / IT / FR / JP sizes.
• Bust, waist and hip (or chest, waist and neck) in cm or inches; every cell is editable, so you can enter your garment's real measurements.
• Copy the size chart as HTML for your product description, copy it as CSV or download a CSV file.

ON-MODEL PHOTOS
• Right-click a product image → "Create an on-model photo with Fashio" opens Fashio's on-model photo tool on fashiolabs.com, a separate paid service. The extension itself uploads nothing.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Checks run on the image files the page serves. Stores often resize photos, so your original upload may be larger.
• Size conversions are standard ready-to-wear conversions; brands vary by up to a full size. Replace them with your garment's own measurements.

PRIVACY
No account, no analytics, no tracking. Pages and images are read inside your browser and nothing is sent to Fashio or anyone else. The only things stored are your chosen marketplace and, on your device, the days you opened the extension (used once to ask whether you would like to rate it).
